A make.fun entry for "Invent a Word We Need" — one invented English word and its one-line definition, for the dread of watching "typing…" for ten minutes and then receiving just "ok".

## The entry

> typok (n.): the dread of watching 'typing…' blink for ten minutes, then getting just 'ok'.

90 characters, 15 words, 1 line. *Typok* is typing + "ok", with a nod to "typo" — the little mistake a one-word reply feels like after a long wait. The word itself is the punchline: ten minutes of blinking dots collapse into the two letters you were waiting for.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 140 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
